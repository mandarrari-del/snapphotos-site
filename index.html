FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Write to support@nexusagencyseo.online.

Do I need a professional camera?

No. Any recent smartphone works. Each mission explains the framing and light it needs, and the AI tells you why a photo was not approved so you can try again.

Why do companies want these photos?

Teams that build and test image-recognition systems need large numbers of real photos of everyday objects, taken in real homes and light. Photos may be included in image collections we license to them. Your name, email and payout details are never included.

How much can I earn?

Each approved photo adds a small reward, usually between $0.05 and $0.30 (up to 2× on Pro). How much you accumulate depends on how many missions you complete and how many are approved. We make no promise of income.

How do withdrawals work?

When your balance reaches $300 you can withdraw to PayPal, Wise or an international bank account in USD. The minimum is the same on every plan and a $2 fee applies to each request.

Is the paid plan a subscription?

No. Plus and Pro are a single payment. There are no recurring charges.

How do I get a refund?

Within 30 days of purchase, request it through the link in your Digistore24 receipt email or write to us with your order ID. See our refund policy.

What photos are not allowed?

Screenshots, images from the internet, duplicates, other people's faces, documents, cards and private screens. Missions are about everyday objects only.
