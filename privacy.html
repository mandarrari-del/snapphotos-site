Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what personal data SnapPhotos (Nexus Management Global, LLC, 7345 W Sand Lake Rd, Suite 210, Office 2345, Orlando, FL 32819, United States) collects, why, and what you can do about it.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Photos: the photos you send for missions, and technical data in them (size, format, and camera data if your phone adds it).
  • Activity: missions done, approvals and rejections, balance, withdrawal requests.
  • Payout data: the payout account you register (for example, a PayPal email).
  • Purchase data: if you buy a plan, Digistore24 shares with us your name, email, the plan and the order status. We never receive your card details.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, used for security and fraud prevention.

2. Photos before you sign up

The first missions can be done before you create an account. Those photos are checked and then discarded; we do not store them. Only the result (approved or not) is kept in your browser until you sign up.

3. How photos are checked

Photos are checked automatically on our own servers by an image-recognition model, to see whether the photo shows what the mission asked for and is not a screenshot or a duplicate. No outside AI company receives your photos for this check.

4. Why we use your data

  • to run your account, review missions and pay withdrawals (contract);
  • to prevent fraud and duplicate accounts (legitimate interest);
  • to meet tax and accounting obligations (legal obligation);
  • to send service emails about your account and withdrawals. Marketing emails only with your consent, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

5. Who we share it with

  • Digistore24 GmbH, for plan purchases and refunds.
  • Our payout provider PayPal, Wise or international bank transfer in US dollars, to send withdrawals.
  • Our hosting provider Hetzner Online GmbH, which stores the data for us.
  • Companies that license photo collections from us. They receive the photos only, never your name, email or payout details.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your account data.

5a. Ranking, activity and community

Inside the app, other members can see your first name and last initial (for example "Rose M."), your plan badge, how much you earned from missions this week, and events such as "joined", "earned $0.40" or "requested a withdrawal". Your email, your photos and your payout details are never shown. Posts and comments you write in the community are visible to all members. You can hide yourself from the ranking and activity at any time in Profile → Privacy.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and activity data: while your account is open, and up to 12 months after closing for fraud and dispute handling.
  • Photos: 24 months after you send them, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Payment and payout records: as long as tax law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and object to some uses. Write to support@nexusagencyseo.online. If you are in the EU/UK you can also complain to your data protection authority; in California you have the rights given by the CCPA, and we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, and access to photos is limited to the people and systems that need it.

9. Children

SnapPhotos is only for people 18 and older. We delete accounts we learn belong to minors.

10. Changes and contact

We will update the date at the top when this policy changes. Questions: support@nexusagencyseo.online.
